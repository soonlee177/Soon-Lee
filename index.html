Profile

A builder of businesses. I start up new units, set up sales and marketing organisations from scratch, and take new high-technology products into markets where they have never been sold.

My career spans renewable energy, semiconductors, software IP licensing, AI, IoT, wearables, telecoms, mobile and wireless. I have opened offices, hired the teams, won the first customers and consistently exceeded targets.

I work comfortably at “C” level — negotiating strategic partnerships with chipset makers, OEMs, ODMs, operators and distributors — and bring a hands-on technical foundation from earlier roles as R&D Head, MIS Head, Systems Consultant and Project Manager.

Fluent in English and Mandarin plus four Chinese dialects, I have lived and worked in Hong Kong, Thailand, Germany, Russia, Taiwan, China and Singapore.
